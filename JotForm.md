I researched Jotform and Wayground against your current app. Your app currently has the core quiz flow: admin login, Gemini topic/document generation, quiz review/edit/publish, live session, QR/join code, player join, timer, answers, leaderboard.

Here are the important things Jotform/Wayground have that your app does not have yet.

Highest Priority

Real dashboard/workspace
Your admin page is one control page. Jotform has a workspace-style product with forms/apps/tables/inbox/workflows. You need a proper dashboard with search, filters, folders, recent quizzes, draft/published/live tabs, duplicate/delete/archive, and quick actions.

Template gallery
Jotform has 20,000+ templates; Wayground has a large resource/library model. Your app has no template browser. Add templates by subject, class, exam type, difficulty, and quiz purpose.

Visual quiz builder
Your review page edits generated questions, but there is no Jotform-style builder. Missing: add question manually, drag/reorder, duplicate question, bulk edit, question-type picker, media picker, preview mode.

Better review/publish workflow
Jotform-style apps clearly separate create, customize, preview, share, and collect. Your flow exists but feels compressed. Add steps like:
Generate -> Review -> Customize -> Preview as player -> Publish -> Launch.

Theme/customization system
Jotform supports styling, themes, colors, layout changes, and custom design controls. Your UI is currently fixed. Add quiz themes, cover images, color presets, fonts, logo/branding, player screen style.

Submission/response management
Jotform has submission management, inbox, tables, export, and reports. Your app has attempts/leaderboard but no admin-facing response table. Add per-participant answers, timestamps, scores, correctness, export, attempt history.

Analytics/report builder
Jotform has visual reports; Wayground has detailed performance reports and AI report analysis. Your app only has a leaderboard. Add question-wise accuracy, participant-wise performance, average score, hardest questions, time taken, downloadable report.

   -----------------

Conditional logic/adaptive flow

Sharing controls
Your app has QR/join code. Missing Jotform-like controls: public/private, password-protected quiz, start/end availability, max participants, reusable link, embed code, custom slug.

Notifications
Jotform supports notification emails and autoresponders. Your app has none. Add admin notification when quiz is completed, participant result email, scheduled reminders.

Medium Priority
11. Integrations
Jotform has integrations with Google Sheets, Slack, Drive, CRM tools, Zapier/webhooks, payment tools, etc. Your app has no integrations. For your use case, start with Google Sheets export, Google Drive import, webhooks, email.

AI assistant beyond generation
Your Gemini service creates/parses questions. Jotform AI can also edit, design, configure settings, generate emails, build conditions, preview/test. Add AI commands like “make these easier,” “add explanations,” “translate to Hindi,” “make 5 more questions,” “detect duplicate questions.”

Import options
You support PDF/DOCX upload. Jotform/Wayground-style flows support broader import. Add paste text, CSV import, Google Docs/Sheets import, image OCR, existing quiz import, URL/webpage import.

Multiple question/content types
Your app is mainly MCQ. Wayground supports many resource/question types. Add true/false, multiple correct, fill in blank, short answer, poll, matching, ordering, image-based question, audio/video question.

Collaboration/team features
Jotform has teams/multiple users. Your app has basic admin creation only. Add teams, roles, ownership, shared quizzes, co-editing or at least shared access.

Workflow/approval
Jotform Workflows support approvals and routing. Your app has draft/publish but no approval. Add “needs review,” reviewer comments, approve/reject, publish permissions.

Save/continue and participant recovery
Jotform supports save and continue later. Your live quiz is session-based. For non-live/self-test mode, add resumable attempts.

Security/compliance basics
Jotform has secure forms, CAPTCHA, password controls, permissions. Your app needs rate limiting, quiz password, anti-spam join protection, basic abuse prevention, better auth/session handling.


AI agents/chatbot
Jotform has AI agents across chat/phone/WhatsApp-style channels. could you add an AI tutor/chat helper for quiz creation or participant explanations.
